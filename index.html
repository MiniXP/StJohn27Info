The villa

On a Clear Day sits above Francis Bay on St. John’s North Shore. Published villa information describes a west-facing home at roughly 170 feet elevation, with four en-suite bedrooms, broad decks, a screened dining porch and a saltwater pool positioned toward the bay and sunset view.

Completed in 2012, the house is designed around indoor-outdoor living: the great room opens toward the pool and Francis Bay, each bedroom has a private balcony, and the outdoor spaces include both an open pool deck and a screened porch for dining.

Bedrooms & layout

The published layout accommodates eight guests. Three bedrooms have king beds; the fourth has two twins that can be configured as a king. Each bedroom has a private bath, balcony, television and individual A/C.

Arrival & Jeep logistics

This guide assumes rental Jeeps for the full stay. Exact ferry schedules and villa arrival instructions should be reconfirmed close to travel.

Step 1

Arrive in St. Thomas

Travel from STT to the appropriate ferry or vehicle-barge departure point based on your Jeep plan.

Step 2

Cross to St. John

The Red Hook–Cruz Bay passenger ferry crossing is roughly 20 minutes; check the current schedule before travel.

Step 3

Pick up / meet your Jeeps

Follow the rental company’s Cruz Bay or St. Thomas pickup instructions.

Step 4

Head to Francis Bay

Use the exact villa directions supplied with the booking. North Shore roads are winding and beach parking can be limited.

Driving basicsDrive on the left

USVI traffic drives on the left. Seat belts are required; parking can be limited at National Park sites and in Cruz Bay.
